# Demo script (about 2.5 minutes)

Back to the [README](README.md). These are speaking notes for the draft demo video. Open the live prototype before recording and close other windows.

**0:00 Who and why (20 s).** "IntraForge is for frontline workers at GMP-regulated manufacturing sites, such as pharma and medtech. They spot improvements every day, but turning an idea into a business case that leadership will act on takes time and paperwork they don't have. IntraForge's AI turns a rough idea into that business case and checks it against the site's SOPs."

**0:20 Central task (45 s).** Click the **Changeover** example, then **Build business case**. "I describe the idea in my own words and pick the area. IntraForge drafts a business case: a summary at the top, the problem, the change, and how the saving was estimated." Point to the dashed figures: "These are the AI's estimates, and I can edit any of them." Open **Show the clause this relies on**: "Each SOP check shows the clause it relies on."

**1:05 A revision and the evidence behind it (40 s).** Click **Filler jams**, then **Build business case**. "In my first version, a vague idea like this hit a dead end. A classmate asked whether it would ask follow-up questions, and another couldn't try it at all because my demo link was missing. Now it asks five quick questions and builds the case from my answers." Fill the answers and build. "It is honest that no SOP matched, and it won't show my share until I add a cost."

**1:45 Where AI fits, and the safeguard (25 s).** Press **Send** without ticking. "It won't send until I confirm I've checked the figures and SOPs. Changing a figure clears that tick." Mention **A risky one**: "An idea that removes a GMP control is returned with a safer alternative. Today the AI is simulated: it matches keywords to prewritten cases. The arithmetic, follow-up questions and review step really work."

**2:10 Limitation and next priority (20 s).** "The main limitation is that the SOP check uses sample clauses, not a real model reading real SOPs. My next priority is to connect a model to a small set of sample SOPs and test whether its citations are right, then test with people who work on shop floors."
