Press Build business case and IntraForge will draft:

  • the problem and the proposed change, in plain terms
  • an estimated annual saving, with every figure editable
  • a check against the site's SOPs, with the clause it relies on
  • a recommended next step, and your share of the saving

If your idea is missing key details, it will ask you for them first. You check the draft before anything goes to leadership.
